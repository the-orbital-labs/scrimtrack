# ScrimTrack Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

ScrimTrack is committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and equitable
community for everyone who participates in good faith. We value thoughtful
collaboration and respect across differences in identity, background,
experience, perspective, and ability.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Communicate with empathy, patience, and respect.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its community.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and make a sincere effort to repair harm.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and requests to stop an unwanted interaction.
- Credit the work and ideas of others appropriately.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or repeated unwanted attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or demeaning comments.
- Discrimination or derogatory statements based on identity or personal
  characteristics.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or attention that is inappropriate for the
  community.
- Threats of violence or encouragement of harm toward any person or group.
- Publishing or sharing another person's private information without explicit
  permission.
- Impersonation or attempts to evade moderation decisions.
- Spam, deceptive promotion, or other conduct that disrupts productive
  participation.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in its
  review.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all ScrimTrack community spaces, including
issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-related
communications. It also applies when someone officially represents ScrimTrack
in public or uses an official project account.

## Reporting a Concern

Report possible violations privately by emailing `hello@scrimtrack.xyz` with
the subject `[ScrimTrack Code of Conduct] Brief description`.

Include relevant links, dates, context, and supporting material when possible.
Do not report sensitive conduct concerns in a public issue or pull request.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with information
shared only when needed to investigate or protect affected people.

Maintainers will acknowledge reports in a timely manner and review them fairly.
Anyone directly involved in a report will not participate in deciding its
outcome when an uninvolved maintainer is available. If no uninvolved maintainer
is available, the project will discuss an appropriate neutral reviewer with
the reporter before sharing confidential details.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.
They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or
other contributions that violate it.

Responses will be based on the nature, severity, and impact of the behavior.
Possible actions include:

1. A private correction explaining the expected behavior.
2. A formal warning or temporary limit on particular interactions.
3. A temporary suspension from project spaces.
4. A permanent ban for severe violations or a pattern of harmful behavior.

Steps may be skipped when necessary to protect the community. Where practical,
enforcement will prioritize accountability, changed behavior, and repair of
harm. Decisions may be appealed through the reporting address with additional
context or evidence.
